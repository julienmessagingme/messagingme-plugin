# Messaging Me for Claude Code

WhatsApp for your app, from Claude Code: your WhatsApp number, an AI agent that answers your customers and a
chat bubble for your site, set up in one session with [Messaging Me](https://app.messagingme.fr/en/developers/).

**No account to create, no key to copy. No WhatsApp number? We provide one. No Meta Business account? Meta's window
creates it along the way, and you can keep your WhatsApp Business app.**

[![The plugin installed, the workspace authorized, the number connected and a first message received, in Claude Code](https://app.messagingme.fr/img/plugin-demo.gif)](https://app.messagingme.fr/en/developers/)

## Install

In Claude Code:

```
/plugin marketplace add messagingme/messagingme-plugin
/plugin install messagingme@messagingme
```

Then type `/mcp`, select `plugin:messagingme:messagingme` and Authenticate: a browser page opens. Continue with Google
(no account yet? your workspace is created right then, with no form) and click Authorize. Nothing to create first. Then:

```
/messagingme:demarrer
```

Claude checks where your workspace stands and takes you through the number, the AI agent, the bubble and a test from
your phone. An interrupted setup resumes where it stopped.

## Which WhatsApp number?

This is where most people get lost, so here are the four words first.

- **Meta Business account**: your business's account at Meta (a "business portfolio", formerly Business Manager), on
  business.facebook.com. It is free; if you advertise on Facebook or Instagram, you probably have one. Otherwise,
  nothing to prepare: with your Facebook profile, Meta's window has you create it during the setup (business name,
  email, country, website).
- **WhatsApp Business app**: the free app for businesses, on your phone, distinct from WhatsApp.
- **API number**: a number connected through Meta to the official WhatsApp API, driven by Messaging Me. It sends your
  messages (confirmations, reminders, campaigns, Shopify messages), and Meta bills them, the templates and the replies
  beyond a monthly allowance, on a payment method you add at Meta.
- **Keeping your WhatsApp Business app**: the number of your WhatsApp Business app also becomes an API number, without
  leaving the app. You keep replying from your phone, and the messages your application sends go out from Messaging
  Me. What you type in the app does not show in Messaging Me yet, and you need to open the app at least every two
  weeks.

Then `/messagingme:demarrer` asks you, one question at a time, the same questions as the
[Get started](https://app.messagingme.fr/en/get-started/) page:

1. **Which plan?** Free, driven entirely from Claude Code, or Pro, which adds the console and includes a provided
   number.
2. **Do you have a Meta Business account?** Yes: your number becomes an API number, an available one of yours or a
   provided one; if it is already in the WhatsApp Business app, you can keep it. No, or not sure: the next question
   (business.facebook.com lists your portfolios, and a missing account is created along the way).
3. **Do you already have the WhatsApp Business app?** Yes: you keep it, no number needed. No: the next question.
4. **Do you have a number for your business?** Yes: download the app, put your number in it, and connect it right
   away. No: we provide one, which goes in the app, its verification code shown on the setup page; you reply to your
   customers from the app from day one. Meta often refuses a brand-new number at first: sending from Messaging Me then
   waits a few days, until you come back to connect it.

The price of a provided number is under [What it costs](#what-it-costs).

## Update

The plugin does not update on its own. In Claude Code: `/plugin`, Marketplaces tab, `messagingme`, "Update marketplace"
(or "Enable auto-update"). In a terminal: `claude plugin update messagingme@messagingme`; on Windows, in PowerShell,
type `claude.cmd` instead of `claude` (PowerShell blocks the `claude.ps1` script). The new version loads in the next
session, or after `/reload-plugins`.

If you had added the same server by hand before (same address, any name), Claude Code keeps yours and sets the
plugin's aside: no duplicate tools, your authorization stays valid. To switch to the plugin's, remove the old one with
`claude mcp remove` followed by its name.

## Data and privacy

The plugin runs no code on your machine: it contains instructions for Claude (skills and a command) and declares one
remote server, the Messaging Me MCP server at `https://api.messagingme.app/mcp`. It talks to nothing else.

- **Authorization.** You sign in to Messaging Me in your browser (OAuth), as an administrator of your workspace. No key
  or secret is stored in your files, and you can revoke the access from the console at any time.
- **What is sent.** When Claude calls a tool at your request, the server receives that call and its arguments (for
  example the text of a message to send, the settings of an AI agent, or the address of a website to import as
  knowledge). It does not receive your conversations with Claude.
- **What comes back.** The data of your workspace that the tool returns: conversations and messages, contacts,
  templates, AI agents, statistics. Actions that change your workspace (sending a message, activating an agent,
  opening a payment page) are marked as such, and payments always happen on Stripe's page, by you.
- **Before you start.** A Messaging Me account (created during the setup). For the official API, a Facebook profile:
  Meta's window offers to create your Meta business portfolio during the setup; messages that Meta bills are charged
  by Meta to your WhatsApp Business account. To keep your WhatsApp Business app, the app on your phone.
